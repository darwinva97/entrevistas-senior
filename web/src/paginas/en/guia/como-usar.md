This site has three kinds of material, and they are meant to be used in a loop, not read front to back.

| Material | What it is | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| [🎤 Mock interviews](/en/entrevistas/) | Full interviews by role and level, with graded answers | To find out where you stand today |
| [🎓 Courses](/en/cursos/) | 10 courses, 44 modules with labs and self-checks | To close the gaps you found |
| [📑 Question bank](/en/banco/) | 389 questions with summary and detailed answers | To practise and to go deep on a topic |

## The loop

1. **Diagnose (1 hour).** Open the [mock interview](/en/entrevistas/) for your role and level. Read **only the questions** and answer out loud, on a timer. Then compare yourself against the three graded answers.
2. **Study.** Every question links to the course module that explains the mechanism. Do the module — including the **lab**, because nothing sticks without running something.
3. **Practise.** Go back to the [bank](/en/banco/) and answer the related questions out loud in 30–60 seconds. If it takes longer, half of it is filler.
4. **Simulate.** Close with a timed mock and score yourself with the rubric at the end of each interview.

## Three rules that make the difference

**Answer out loud, on a timer.** A correct four-minute answer is a failed answer. The bank's format — summary plus detail — exists for that: the summary is what you *say*, the detail is what supports the follow-ups.

**Do the labs.** You cannot sound credible about a GC pause, a `pprof` profile or a Kafka rebalance if you have never seen the real output. Ten minutes in a terminal beat an hour of reading.

**Study the mistakes, not just the right answers.** Every module has a "common mistakes" section and every mock interview has a ❌ answer. Those are the exact phrases that make an interviewer downgrade you — and most of them sound perfectly reasonable.

> **The three-level rule:** for any topic you should be able to answer in 30 seconds (what it is), in 3 minutes (how it works internally and its trade-offs), and with a story of your own (when you used it or broke it). If the third level is missing, build the lab that gives it to you.

## Where to start depending on your time

- **You have an interview next week:** the [10-day sprint](/en/guia/plan-de-estudio/) — and if you only have three days, distributed-systems fundamentals plus your STAR stories.
- **You want to change league, not pass an exam:** the 8-week track in the same study plan.
- **You just want to check where you stand:** one mock interview for your role, timed, recorded.

## A note on language

The mock interviews, this guide and the study plan are fully available in English. The **question bank** and the **courses** are still in Spanish: you will see them here with a notice at the top of the page. Translations are welcome — the content lives as plain Markdown in the [repository](https://github.com/darwinva97/entrevistas-senior).
