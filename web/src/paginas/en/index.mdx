## What a graded answer looks like

In the [mock interviews](/en/entrevistas/), every question is answered in four layers:

> **❌ What NOT to say** — the plausible-sounding answer that drops you a level, and why it is wrong.
>
> **⚠️ Acceptable answer** — the one that passes but does not stand out, and exactly what it is missing.
>
> **✅ Ideal answer** — the one that moves you up a band: mechanism, trade-off and a number.
>
> **🔁 Likely follow-up** — what they will ask next, and how you handle it.

Because a senior interview rarely scores you on being right: it scores **how you justify**, which trade-offs you see, and what you are careful not to claim.
