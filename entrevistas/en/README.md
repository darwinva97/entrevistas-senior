# 🎤 Mock interviews by role and level

Complete interviews, written the way a real interviewer runs them, with **graded answers**: what not to say, what merely passes, and what stands out.

## How every question is answered

Each question in this section has the same structure, because senior interviews rarely score you on being right — they score **how you justify** and what you are careful not to claim.

| Layer | What it is | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| **❌ What NOT to say** | The plausible answer that drops you a level, and exactly why it is wrong | Spotting your own reflexes |
| **⚠️ Acceptable answer** | The one that passes but does not stand out, and what it is missing | Knowing where you are today |
| **✅ Ideal answer** | The one that moves you up a band: mechanism, trade-off, number | Knowing where you are going |
| **🔁 Likely follow-up** | What they ask next, and how you handle it | Not collapsing in round two |

> **These answers are written to be said out loud.** If an "ideal" one takes you more than 90 seconds, cut half of it: give the headline, offer to go deeper, and let the interviewer choose.

## How to use this section

1. **Pick your role and level** (or the level you are aiming for: train one band above).
2. **Read the question only.** Answer out loud, on a timer, before looking at anything.
3. **Compare yourself against the three layers.** Most people find themselves in the ⚠️: correct, but with no mechanism and no trade-off.
4. **Mark every ❌ you have actually said.** Those are your reflexes, and they are the first thing to unlearn.
5. **Close the loop:** every question links to the course or bank question with the full theory.

## Level conventions

| Level | What they expect from your answer |
|---|---|
| **Junior** | Correct fundamentals, honesty about what you don't know, demonstrable curiosity |
| **Mid** | Autonomy: you solve things unsupervised and know the classic traps |
| **Senior** | Trade-offs, data, production impact, deciding with incomplete information |
| **Staff / Lead / Architect** | Scope beyond your team: systems, process, people, cost and risk |

Levels are not universal: what one company calls *senior*, another calls *mid*. Use the table for calibration, not as a label.
